Add several pieces at once

Handy when you have a batch of new work. Fill in your pieces here, download one file, and upload it in your Puppet Vendors account. Your pieces arrive as drafts, ready for photos. For one or two pieces, Create Product in your account is quicker.

  1. Choose your name and fill in your pieces.
  2. Click Download upload file at the bottom.
  3. In your account, go to Products → Import and upload the file.
  4. Open each draft, add photos, set it to Active and click Submit.

Not sure which options to pick? See the Shop Guide. Your list is saved on this device until you clear it.

How to upload the file
  1. Log in to your Puppet Vendors account and open Products.
  2. Click Import, choose the file you downloaded, and upload it.
  3. If any rows are marked in red, hover over the red box to see why, fix it here, and download again.
  4. Your pieces appear in Products as drafts. Open each one, add photos, set it to Active, and click Submit at the bottom.

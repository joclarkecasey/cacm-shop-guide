Adding your work to the CACM Shop

Everything you list in your artist account appears in the online shop at shop.cacm.studio and on your own artist page. Here’s how to log in, add a piece, and keep your listings up to date.

In the gallery, online and shipping

Everything you list is for sale online. Two dropdowns tell shoppers, and the gallery, where your piece is and how the buyer gets it.

Availability: where the piece is

On display in Gallery Boutique
It’s in the gallery. Shoppers can see it in person or buy it online. If it sells online, the gallery sets it aside for the buyer. Nothing for you to do.
Online only
It’s with you, not in the gallery. If it sells, bring it to the gallery within 3 days, labelled with the order number.

Pickup & shipping: how the buyer gets it

Gallery pickup only
The buyer collects it from the gallery.
Gallery pickup or shipping on request
The buyer still checks out with pickup. If they’d rather have it shipped, they ask CACM and we put them in touch with you to arrange and charge for shipping.

Swapping pieces in and out of the boutique

When you take a piece out of the boutique, or bring a new one in, update it in your account the same day so the shop always matches what’s on the shelves:

  • Taking a piece home: change Availability to Online only, or set it to Draft if it’s no longer for sale.
  • Bringing a piece in: change Availability to On display in Gallery Boutique. If it’s new, add it first.

Then click Submit at the bottom. If the shop says a piece is in the gallery when it isn’t, a buyer may come to collect something that isn’t there.

Every online order is a gallery pickup. The buyer gets an email when it’s ready to collect. Shipping is only ever arranged afterwards, between you and the buyer.

Add a new piece

Allow about 10 minutes for your first listing. Have your photos and measurements ready before you start.

  1. 1

    Log in

    Open the login page and enter your email and password. Bookmark the page and save your password: you’ll need both every time you add or change a piece.

    You already have an account, so never click Sign up. That sends a new request instead of logging you in.

    Forgotten your password? Click Forgot your password? and choose a new one. Can’t find your welcome email? Check spam for support@puppetvendors.com.

  2. 2

    Click Create Product

    Go to Products in the top menu, then click the blue + Create Product button.

  3. 3

    Title and description

    Title: the piece’s name and what it is, e.g. “Tidepool Bowl”. No prices or ALL CAPS, and leave out your name (it’s added for you).

    Description: 2–4 short sentences. What it is, how you made it, and what makes it special. Shoppers can’t pick it up, so your words do that work.

  4. 4

    Product type and tags

    On the right, pick a Product type:

    • Functional: made to be used (mugs, bowls, vases)
    • Decorative: looks useful but isn’t for use
    • Sculptural: made to be looked at
    • Jewelery: earrings, pendants, beads

    Add 3–6 Tags shoppers might search for, like the colour or use: blue, bowl, serving. Press Enter after each one.

  5. 5

    Add your photos

    Drag 3–5 photos into the box, or click browse. Photos straight from your phone are fine.

    • Square crops look best in the shop
    • Plain background, soft daylight
    • Front, another angle, and a close-up

    The first photo is the one shown in the shop, so make it your best front view.

  6. 6

    Price, weight and quantity

    Price: whole dollars, no $ sign, e.g. 85.

    Weight: the piece on its own, no packaging. A kitchen scale is fine.

    Quantity: 1 for a one-of-a-kind piece. Keep Track quantity ticked so it shows Sold out once it sells.

    The SKU fills in by itself. Leave it as it is, and copy it onto the piece’s tag for the gallery till.

  7. 7

    Fill in every detail

    Height, width, depth: inches, numbers only, at the widest point.

    Materials: clay body first, then glaze, e.g. “Stoneware, blue glaze”.

    Firing type, Availability, Pickup & shipping: choose from the dropdowns. See In the gallery, online and shipping if you’re not sure which to pick.

    Care: short and practical, e.g. “Food safe, hand wash”. Low fire luster, raku and pit fired work is never food safe.

  8. 8

    Set it to Active

    Back at the top right, change Publish Status to Active when the listing is ready to go live.

    Still working on it? Leave it on Draft. Drafts don’t show in the shop.

  9. 9

    Scroll down and click Submit

    Nothing is saved until you click Submit.

    The button is at the very bottom right of the form and easy to miss. Click it once, then wait.

    If saving seems slow, wait a moment and refresh the page. Don’t click Submit again, as that can make a duplicate.

  10. 10

    Check your list

    You’re back on Products, with your new piece in the list and its status showing. Click Edit any time to change it.

Keep it up to date

Open any piece from Products with Edit, make your change, and click Submit at the bottom. Do this whenever a piece sells elsewhere, or moves in or out of the boutique (see Swapping pieces).

  1. 11

    When a piece sells

    Sales online and at the gallery till update by themselves. If you sell a piece another way, set Quantity to 0 and Submit. It will show as Sold out.

    To take a piece off the shop completely, set its status to Draft.

    Please don’t delete sold pieces. Keeping them keeps a record of the sale.

  2. 12

    Feature a piece on the homepage

    Add the tag Featured, spelled exactly like that, and Submit. The piece then appears in Featured products on the shop’s homepage.

    Please feature just one piece at a time. If it sells, choose another.

  3. 13

    One piece in several designs

    Making the same piece in different glazes or designs, like ornaments or small dishes? List them together so shoppers choose from a menu.

    • Tick Variants
    • Name the option Design and add each one: Blue, Green…
    • For each design, click + to choose its photo, then add its price, weight and quantity

    Keep one-of-a-kind pieces as their own listings.

See it in the shop

  1. 14

    Find your artist page

    Go to shop.cacm.studio, click Artists, then your name. Your active pieces appear under your photo and bio.

    Want to update your bio or photo? Use the artist page form.

Getting paid

You don’t need to do anything to get paid. Commission is worked out automatically for every sale.

Commission: 20% for sales through the online shop, 10% for sales at the gallery till.

Payouts: monthly, by Venmo, for the previous month’s sales.

Checking your sales: the Reports and Payouts tabs in your account show each sale, the commission, and what you’ve been paid.

When something sells

You’ll get an email. If the piece is already at the gallery, there’s nothing to do: the gallery handles pickup. If it’s an online-only piece, bring it in within 3 days, labelled with the order number. If the buyer asks for shipping, CACM will put you in touch. Please don’t click Fulfill: the gallery marks orders as ready and collected.

You don’t need to use Orders or Fulfillments in your account. The gallery handles them.
